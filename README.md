### About me

I'm an Android developer who enjoys understanding how things work beyond the UI: performance, tooling, app-to-app communication, and the constraints of real devices. I work primarily with **Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose, and Android tooling**, and I'm interested in AI integration and on-device computing.
